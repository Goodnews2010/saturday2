Admission Information

Excellent Heights Academy welcomes applications from parents and guardians who wish to enrol their children.

Admission Requirements

  • Completed admission application form.
  • Recent passport photograph.
  • Birth certificate.
  • Previous school report, where applicable.
  • Medical information, where required.

Application Procedures

  1. Collect or complete the admission form.
  2. Fill in the student's information.
  3. Submit the required documents.
  4. Attend the admission assessment if required.
  5. Receive admission confirmation.

Application Deadline: December 15, 2026 (sample date).
