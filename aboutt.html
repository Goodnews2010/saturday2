About Our School

Excellent Heights Academy is a private school established to provide quality education and develop responsible future leaders.

Our school provides a safe and friendly environment where students can learn, grow and achieve their goals.

Our History

Excellent Heights Academy was established with the aim of providing affordable and quality education. Since its establishment, the school has continued to encourage academic excellence, discipline and creativity.

Our Mission

To provide quality education that develops students intellectually, morally and socially.

Our Vision

To become a leading educational institution known for excellence, discipline and innovation.

Our Core Values

  • Discipline
  • Honesty
  • Respect
  • Hard work
  • Excellence
  • Creativity

Our School Philosophy

We believe that every child has the ability to learn and succeed. We encourage practical learning, good behaviour, teamwork and personal development.
