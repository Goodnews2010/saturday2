Our Academic Programmes

Excellent Heights Academy provides education at different levels to prepare students for the future.

1. Nursery Education

We provide early childhood education that helps children develop basic learning skills.

2. Primary Education

Our primary programme builds strong foundations in reading, writing, mathematics and other subjects.

3. Junior Secondary Education

Students are introduced to different subjects and practical learning activities.

4. Senior Secondary Education

We prepare students for external examinations and higher educational opportunities.
