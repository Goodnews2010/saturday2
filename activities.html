Our School Activities

We encourage students to participate in extracurricular activities to develop their talents, confidence and teamwork.

1. Football

Football helps students develop teamwork, discipline and physical fitness.

2. Basketball

Students learn teamwork, coordination and sportsmanship.

3. Debate Club

The debate club develops public speaking and critical thinking skills.

4. Music Club

Students explore their musical talents through singing and instruments.

5. Coding Club

Students learn basic programming and develop problem-solving skills.

6. Cultural Day

Students celebrate different cultures, traditions and languages.

School Events

  • Inter-house sports competition.
  • Annual cultural day.
  • Graduation ceremony.
  • Science exhibition.
  • School debate competition.
